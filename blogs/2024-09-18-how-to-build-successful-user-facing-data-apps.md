Building good dashboards for internal users is not easy, but the challenges and pitfalls are generally well understood . Creating data apps for external users is an entirely different matter, with its own set of considerations. User-facing analytics are data apps and dashboards that are made for users external to your organization, usually your customers, clients, or end-users.
