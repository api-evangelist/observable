Newsrooms have long been at the forefront of data visualization, turning messy datasets into clear, powerful stories that resonate with broad audiences. On December 4th, leading data journalists, Jared Whalen (Polygraph/The Pudding), Ben Welsh (Reuters), and Kavya Beheraj (Axios), gave us a peek behind the curtain on their process. The hour-long discussion covered topics like knowing if there’s a story worth telling in the data, collaborating with others, and tips for communicating with clarity, urgency, and impact for the broader data visualization community.
