In July 2024, as U.S. election news took a dramatic turn, one Observable Notebook surged in popularity: the ActBlue Ticker Tracker . Created by Ryan Murphy , this dashboard accesses and visualizes contributions to ActBlue for “democratic candidates and progressive causes,” as reported by the ActBlue website .
