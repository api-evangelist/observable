Most data analysts cut their teeth on a familiar set of chart types when learning data visualization skills — among them, the bar chart, the line chart, the pie chart, and the scatter plot. These standard charts are widely understood, easy to build, and dependable for many common analyses. They come pre-built in most business intelligence or AI for analysis tools, and are often the first chart types that data teams use for exploring trends and patterns in a dataset.
