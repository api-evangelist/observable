Databases are the main way that large organizations store data that needs to be analyzed. But databases don’t just store the data, they also allow you to work with it: ask questions by running queries, create new combinations of facts by joining tables , all to understand what is in the data and to better inform business decisions. In this post we cover some basics of databases including core terminology, an introduction to tables (and how data is structured in them), and the basics of keys and joins.
