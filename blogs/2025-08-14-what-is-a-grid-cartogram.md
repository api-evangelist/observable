What if you could take a map, ignore all the messy borders, and turn it into a clean, tile-based layout — without losing its geographic meaning? That’s the idea behind grid cartograms, an engaging and powerful way to visualize spatial data. Instead of using traditional map shapes, grid cartograms represent regions like states or countries with uniform geometric tiles, arranged in a roughly geographic layout.
