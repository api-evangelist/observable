Data visualization sits at a crossroads: part analytical discipline, part creative craft. On Nov 11, we hosted Art + science: Blending creativity and analysis in data visualization, a webinar on how three data visualization leaders — Kelsey Nanan , Tanya Shapiro , and Shirley Wu — strike that balance in their work. What transpired was an engaging discussion covering fundamentals, such as chart choices and collaboration, and the more abstract, such as how personal backgrounds influence data visualization work, creative risk-taking, and even the future of AI in data visualization.
